# Audit, 4 September 2026

An adversarial correctness and security review of v1, run the same day it was built, and what changed as a result. Kept here so the next reviewer can see what was already found.

## Fixed

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Any signed-in user could reach a crew's secret invite link by guessing its slug, because non-members were redirected to `/join/<token>`. | Non-members now get a 404. The invite link is only shown inside the crew. |
| 2 | High | Sign-in codes used `Math.random`, allowed unlimited guesses, and every request added another live code. | `crypto.randomInt`; a new code invalidates earlier ones; five wrong guesses burn the code; at most three live codes per address. |
| 3 | Medium | Open redirect: `/signin?next=//evil.com` bounced signed-in users off-site. | One `safeNext` validator (rejects `//` and `/\`) used by the page and every action. Unit tested. |
| 4 | Medium | The only organiser could leave, orphaning the crew. | Last organiser cannot leave or be demoted. |
| 5 | Medium | Removed members kept their RSVP rows: phantom headcounts, hidden no-show charges. | Leaving or removal frees held spots on open sessions (not a late drop) and clears reserve rows, in a transaction. |
| 6 | Medium | Re-confirming attendance turned an un-ticked walk-on into a no-show with a charge. | Walk-ons are attendance rows only. The table counts a confirmed attendance row as played even without an RSVP. Unit tested. |
| 7 | Medium | Dropping out after kick-off promoted a reserve into a game that had already happened. | No RSVP changes of any kind after kick-off. Unit tested. |
| 8 | Medium | RSVP, attendance and rating writes were not transactional: two taps could both win the last spot; a mid-way failure could wipe votes. | Immediate transactions on rsvp, confirm, rate, capacity change and member removal. |
| 9 | Low–Med | A played session could be cancelled, leaving its charges in the ledger. | Only open sessions can be cancelled. |
| 10 | Low | "Count me in" checkbox on the pin form was ignored. | Fixed. |
| 11 | Low | Organiser-supplied ids (on-behalf RSVP, attendance ticks, payment session) were not checked against the crew. | All checked against membership or crew ownership. |
| 12 | Low | Email delivery failure still redirected to "check your inbox". | Surfaces an error unless running in console-code dev mode. |
| 13 | Low | Plain form actions threw on forged requests. | Wrapped so expected errors are swallowed rather than crashing the page. |
| 14 | Low | `datetime-local` could emit `24:00` on some ICU builds; predictor lock was frozen at setup time. | `hourCycle: h23` plus a guard, tested across BST/GMT; the lock now follows the session's start time. |
| 15 | Low | Americano rounds unbounded; predictions accepted after a result was entered. | Bounded 1–12; closed once a result exists. |

UX items fixed in the same pass: on-behalf RSVP buttons no longer say "You're in" about someone else; tapping in no longer reads as "joined the crew" in the feed; "Played" list renamed "Past"; a session with nobody in shows the booking total rather than "£65 each"; members see "Awaiting result" instead of an organiser-only "Needs confirming"; player pages carry the player's name in the title.

## Accepted for now

- **Share-card images are public by design.** They show first names, counts and the player of the match. Ids are 14 random characters, so links are unguessable, and the invite-link leak above was the only route to discovering them. Nothing more sensitive goes on a card.
- **Guest account plus existing email.** A guest who signs in with an email that already has an account is switched to that account; the guest's memberships are not merged. Rare in a pilot; a merge flow is on the roadmap.
- **No IP-level rate limiting.** Codes are throttled per address. Put the app behind a proxy or edge rule with IP limits before a public launch.
- **Expired sessions and codes are never purged.** Harmless at pilot scale; add a nightly job with the first cron.
- **Rater count for form** is inferred from vote totals rather than stored. Add a raters table when the third rating category becomes optional.

## Test coverage after the audit

40 unit tests (domain rules, redirect validation, London-time round-trips including the clocks going back) and the end-to-end run of the full loop as three people.
