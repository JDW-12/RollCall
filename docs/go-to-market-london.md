# Go-to-Market: London

Status: working draft, September 2026. This is the plan for the first 12 weeks. Numbers marked "assumption" are targets, not measured results.

## The first 15 crews

We win London one organiser at a time. The target is 15 crews live by the end of week 4 of the pilot, spread deliberately so we learn across sports and not just from one Powerleague league.

| Sport | Target crews | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 5/7-a-side football | 6 | Largest segment, worst dropout problem, highest money per session |
| Padel | 4 | Fastest growing, peak-slot scarcity makes reliable crews valuable to venues |
| Golf society or fourball | 2 | Tests the Stableford card and lower session frequency |
| Gym crew | 2 | Tests the loop without a venue cost |
| Race-weekend crew | 1 | Tests the podium predictor and watching-together mode |

Every one of the 15 must have an organiser who has run the group for at least three months on WhatsApp. We are not creating crews; we are moving existing ones.

## Where to find organisers

- **Powerleague and Goals leagues.** Team captains in the Tuesday and Thursday adult leagues at sites such as Shoreditch, Mill Hill, Wembley and Beckenham. Captains already collect money and chase numbers every week. Approach in person after games, not by email.
- **Rocket Padel, Padium and Padel Social Club.** Regular fours at peak slots. Ask front desk staff who books the same slot every week. These are the crews venues care about most.
- **Footy Addicts regulars who want their own group.** People who play the same drop-in game every week and complain about fees in the reviews. They are one step from starting a fixed crew and Roll Call gives them a reason.
- **University alumni groups.** Old college football and hockey groups that still play, typically organised by one person who never handed it over.
- **Workplace teams.** Company 5-a-side and padel groups, usually with a Slack channel and a social committee budget.
- **Run-club adjacent crews.** The strength or padel group that forms around a Saturday run club. Run clubs themselves are well served by Strava; the spin-off crews are not.

The growth lead owns this list and visits at least four venues a week during the pilot.

## The organiser-first pitch

Keep it under a minute. Lead with the pain, not the product.

> "You run the Tuesday game, right? How often does someone drop the night before and leave you short? Roll Call fixes that. You pin the session once, everyone taps in from a link, no app to install. If someone drops inside 24 hours they still owe their share, and the reserve list fills the spot automatically. After the game everyone votes player of the match in three taps and it builds a league table for the season. It costs nothing. Want me to set up your next session with you now?"

Set up the first session with them on the spot, on their phone. Send the share card into their WhatsApp group while you are standing there. Do not leave it as a link to try later.

## Referral loop via share cards

The share card is the growth mechanic. Every session pin, every league table update and every player card is an image with a link that renders as a preview in WhatsApp. Members see it without installing anything. Each member is in other crews, and the card carries a "start your own crew" action.

Target for the pilot (assumption): at least one new crew started by a member of an existing pilot crew for every three pilot crews, by week 12.

We measure share card sends per session and new crews attributed to a share card. If cards are not being sent, the design is wrong and we fix the card before anything else.

## Venue partnership pitch

Venues have two problems: peak slots are oversubscribed (85 per cent peak occupancy in padel, with about half of players struggling to book), and off-peak slots sit empty. A Roll Call crew with a visible turn-up rate solves both. The pitch to a venue manager:

> "We can show you crews that have turned up to 90 per cent of their sessions this season. Give them a standing weekly slot and they will fill it. Give them a discount on your Tuesday 2pm dead time and they will fill that too. We take no booking fee and we do not replace your booking system."

In the pilot we ask for nothing in return except introductions to regular crews and a poster at the front desk. Commercial terms (referral fee, standing slot placement) come in v2 once we have retention data to show.

## Content plan

Two channels, TikTok and Instagram, run by the growth lead with the designer. The content is the product output, not adverts about the product.

| Format | Cadence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Season awards reels (MOTM, grafter, worst miss, top Sick Note) | End of each pilot crew's season | Auto-generated from crew data with consent from the crew |
| Sick Notes leaderboard | Weekly | Anonymised or with crew consent, pinned to the pilot crews' own chats |
| Player card reveals | As they update | Members share their own; we repost with permission |
| Organiser stories | Fortnightly | Short interviews with pilot organisers about what changed |

No paid promotion in the pilot. Everything we post should be something a crew member would send to the group chat anyway.

## Pilot success criteria (12 weeks)

The pilot passes if, at week 12:

- At least 11 of the 15 crews are still pinning sessions without a prompt from us.
- Median organiser pins a session in under 60 seconds.
- At least 60 per cent of invited members tap in within 48 hours of a pin.
- Turn-up rate across pilot crews is above 85 per cent (assumption for target).
- Rating completion rate after sessions is above 50 per cent (assumption for target).
- At least five new crews started from share cards sent by pilot crews.
- Zero unresolved money disputes escalated to us.

If fewer than 8 crews are still active at week 12, we stop and rework the core loop before spending anything further on growth. Definitions and formulas are in metrics.md.

## What we would not do

- **No paid ads before retention.** Buying crews that churn at week 4 teaches us nothing and burns runway.
- **No player-finder for strangers.** That is Footy Addicts' and Playtomic's job and their reviews show what it costs in fee and refund disputes. Roll Call is for people who already know each other.
- **No booking integration in v1.** Booking belongs to venues and their platforms (Playtomic, Playfinder). We link out later; we do not build it now.
- **No prizes on the predictor.** Free-to-enter, no prizes, so it stays outside the Gambling Act. See legal-and-compliance.md.
- **No venue exclusivity deals.** We want to be neutral so any venue will work with us.
- **No expansion outside London until 300 active crews.** Density matters for venue partnerships and for the referral loop.
